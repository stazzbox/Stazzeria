Independent Office

Head of Stazz is not another name for the Stazzerian President. Its institutional responsibilities concern the Stazz Universe and Stazzex-directed administration; the office reports to the Stazzex rather than to the Presidency.

Stazzerian Interpretation

Within Stazzerian terminology, Head of Stazz has been described as its distinctive head-of-state equivalent. This is not a claim to the conventional powers or status of a sovereign state's head of state.

Concurrent Appointment

Kelvin Stazzland currently holds both Head of Stazz and President of Stazzeria. The offices retain independent definitions and may have different holders in the future.
