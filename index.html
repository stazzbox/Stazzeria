01 · History of Stazzeria

The Stazzerian Presidential Timeline records the development of the Stazzerian Castle, the Presidency, and the Department of Stazz. Read chronologically, these milestones trace the movement from early political beginnings into a formal national framework and the succession of its executive offices.

April 15, 2017

Founding of Stazzeria

Stazzeria is founded, establishing the origin of the Stazzerian collective and its subsequent cultural and institutional development.

Origins2022 · The Stazzerian Castle
March 23, 2022

Beginning of the Stazzerian Party Campaign

The Stazzerian Party Campaign begins, establishing an early political milestone that precedes the formation of the Stazzerian Castle.

April 13, 2022 – January 2025

Stazzerian Castle Formation and Takeover Era

The Stazzerian Castle (STX-079) concept forms on April 13, 2022, with Amy and Kelvin Stazzland associated with this foundational milestone. This date also marks the beginning of Kelvin Stazzland’s first presidential term. Beginning December 20, 2022, Amy and Kelvin grow in prominence as a power couple, united by their shared life agenda and influence within their social environment. The term “Takeover” reflects their collective rise and influence rather than a hostile seizure. Amy’s foundational distinction is preserved through the Original StazzQueen (OSQ, STX-077) designation, derived from StazzQueen to identify her as its original and foundational holder. OSQ recognizes Amy’s role in Kelvin’s life and her significance as the foundational model for the StazzQueen role, including the concepts of The Stazzian Estate and The Stazzerian Castle. The era concludes in January 2025.

National Development2024 · Formal Establishment
2024

Formal Establishment of Stazzeria

Founded April 15, 2017, Stazzeria was formally established as an organized nation and collective in 2024. Its physical expression is formed through in-person gatherings of Stazzerians, while its digital republic is sustained through the Stazzex.

Presidential Succession2025 · Second Term and Institutional Change
February 1, 2025

End of the First Term; Beginning of the Second

Kelvin Stazzland’s first presidential term ends and his second term begins. Amy Stazzland’s First Ladyship also concludes on this date. Caro serves as Vice President at the beginning of the second term.

September 14, 2025

Vice Presidential Succession

Isaac Ochoa succeeds Caro as Vice President of Stazzeria. Concurrently, Caro transitions into the office of Stazzerian Advisor, continuing to serve the administration in a strategic advisory capacity.

October 2025

The Zits Conflict

Stazzeria faces the Zits Conflict after Zits challenges the collective. The recorded account describes Kelvin Stazzland and John B. as victorious.

October 31, 2025

LeAnn Stazzland Becomes First Lady

Following the earlier conclusion of Amy Stazzland’s tenure, LeAnn Stazzland assumes the First Ladyship and becomes Head Stazzlie and the leading feminine representative of Stazzeria.

Early November 2025

John B. Promoted to War General

In the period following the Zits Conflict, John B. is promoted to War General, formalizing his position as Stazzeria’s principal military and conflict authority.

Current Era2026–Present · Institutional Expansion
2026–Present

Institutional Expansion Era

Stazzeria continues to develop its government, institutional structure, and national records. The Stazzex preserves this continuity through its archival infrastructure, including leadership records, national history, cultural memory, and institutional documentation.

Historical scope: Stazzerian national and governmental milestones. Unrelated Stazz Universe events are excluded. The timeline records the interval between Amy Stazzland’s First Ladyship ending and LeAnn Stazzland’s tenure beginning without assigning an additional designation to that period.

President

The Presidency (STX-050) is the central executive office responsible for national direction, sovereignty, order, unity, cultural continuity, and Stazzerian identity.

“STX 049 — Stazzerian Vice President”

Vice President

The Vice President (STX-049) is the principal executive counterpart to the Presidency, providing continuity, coordination, representation, and executive support.

“STX 014 — First Lady of Stazzeria”

First Lady

LeAnn Stazzland is the current First Lady, Head Stazzlie, leading female representative of the NATION, and presiding ceremonial authority of the Stazzlies. Her responsibilities include presiding over and representing the Stazzlies, supporting cultural continuity, public representation, Stazzerian events and symbolism, and presidential initiatives.

“STX 048 — Stazzerian War General”

War General

The War General (STX-048) is the primary military and conflict authority responsible for strategic protection, rapid conflict response, security alignment, internal stability, operational defense, and defensive continuity. John B. Stazzland currently holds the office.

“STX 047 — Stazzerian Judicial Reference”

First Judge

The First Judge (STX-047) is the senior judicial office associated with the Stazziciary. James C currently serves as Interim First Judge while also holding the Mediator role. The SSAC is the formal court responsible for hearing and adjudicating matters under the Stazzerian Framework.

“STX 039 — Stazzerian Advisor”

Advisor

The Advisor (STX-039) provides senior strategic counsel, institutional continuity, and high-level guidance to the Presidency. Caro currently holds the office and previously served as first-term Vice President.

“STX 046 — Stazzerian Monarch”

Monarch

The Monarch (STX-046) is a distinct office in the Stazzerian hierarchy, with the recognized Monarch/Jester institutional dynamic.
