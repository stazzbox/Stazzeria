Mediator of Stazzeria

James C.

The Mediator facilitates communication and addresses institutional challenges involving Stazzeria, its President, Department of Stazz, and citizens. Mediation is distinct from judicial adjudication.

Office & Responsibilities

The Mediator serves as a channel for dialogue and resolution of challenges within the Stazzerian institutional framework. The role does not supersede presidential or judicial authority.

Concurrent Appointment

James C. also serves as Interim First Judge. The two appointments remain institutionally separate.

Loyalty & Brotherhood

Beyond their respective offices, Mediator James C. and President Kelvin Stazzland share a bond of loyalty, brotherhood, and mutual respect. This personal relationship is acknowledged without altering the responsibilities of either office.
