President of Stazzeria

Kelvin Stazzland

The President is the principal executive authority of Stazzeria and coordinates its direction under Stazzerian Presidential Collectivism. The Presidency is distinct from the office of Head of Stazz.

Presidential Office

The Stazzerian President serves as executive head of Stazzeria under its own institutional framework. The Presidency is separate from the office of Head of Stazz, even when one person holds both appointments.

Presidential History

Kelvin Stazzland's first term began April 13, 2022, associated with the formation of the Stazzerian Castle. The second term began February 1, 2025. The national portal maintains the fuller historical timeline and succession record.

Concurrent Appointment

Kelvin Stazzland concurrently serves as Head of Stazz. This is a fact about the current officeholder, not part of the definition of the Presidency.
